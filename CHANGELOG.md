## 0.2.0

- Add Granite Speech 5.0 2B (4-bit, on MLX) as a speech model option.

## 0.1.0-alpha

- Plan writing with outlines and a 2d canvas.
- Sources (webpage, pdf, ebook, text, image) alongside writing.
- Dictate and transcribe with macOS or Parakeet.
- Check spelling and grammar with Harper.
- Alternative wordings and text dimming in the editor, plus rich text features.
- Export to Markdown, text, HTML, EPUB, .odt, .docx and .pdf.
- Choose light or dark themes with optional backgrounds.
